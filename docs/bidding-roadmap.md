# Bidding Coverage Roadmap

This is a short list of work remaining after introducing all-seat practice
and before final project cleanup. The executable bidding rules, practice corpus, and README
describe the selected bidding system and its deliberate scope limits; this file is
not a historical progress log or a commitment to model every possible auction.

## Agreed scope

- Generally teach openings, responses, opener rebids, and selected responder rebids;
  and overcalls/Doubles, advances, and selected overcaller/Doubler rebids. Later
  judgment-heavy bidding may end with auction-specific guidance.
- Do not create practice exercises merely to achieve numerical rule coverage.
  Preserve credible alternatives and prioritize useful instructional decisions.
- Use the same selected bidding agreements for all four seats, including the
  compact fixed DONT treatment against a strong 1NT opening.
- Practice deals have four fixed, complete hands and credible recorded calls for
  every seat. North is always dealer, but need not be opener. Exercise completion
  need not mean the actual bridge auction is over.

## Completed interaction work

- [x] **All-seat practice interaction.** Every seat's recorded call, including early
  Passes, now requires a student entry. A different legal call receives feedback,
  then the recorded call is used to preserve the lesson. Exercises stop at the
  recorded final call, even if the real bridge auction would continue.

## Instructional explanation review

- [x] **Initial source-based instructional census and review queue.** Inventoried rule
  declarations by stage, identified existing dedicated explanation families and shared
  meaning/guidance, and prioritized meaningful potential teaching gaps. See
  [Instructional Explanation Coverage Review](instructional-explanation-review.md).
  This is **not** per-rule approval and does not imply every case has been played through.
- [ ] **Validate prior direct overcall, takeout Double, and balancing explanations (B4–B8).**
  Check ordinary and weak-two Double variants, natural suit/NT and stopper tests,
  preemptive jumps, DONT choices, fourth-seat balancing, plausible rejections and
  deliberate judgment boundaries. Record when existing meaning/guidance is adequate.
- [ ] **Validate selected earlier response/advancing corners (B9–B20).** Especially
  agreements after 1NT/2NT and strong 2C, weak preempt defense/advancing,
  Jordan 2NT/Redouble, competitive negative/responsive Doubles, and unmodeled high
  interference. Avoid reworking already-good first actions simply for rule coverage.
- [ ] **Deep review and improve selected overcaller/takeout-Doubler rebids.** Include
  natural 1NT overcall conventions/invitations, natural suit overcall advances and
  later competition, ordinary/strong takeout-Doubler actions, and weak-two defense
  continuations. Contrast actual strength, fit, stopper, forcing status and alternatives.
- [ ] **Deep review and improve selected responder rebids.** Include NT conventions,
  strong 2C/Jacoby/splinters, Standard versus 2/1 forcing sequences, delayed support,
  economical descriptive suits, simple raises/game tries, and judgment boundaries.
- [ ] **Final opener-rebid verification.** Extensive explanation work is complete,
  but audit the remaining corners (particularly NT-opening interference, jump-overcall
  continuations and forcing end states). Do not equate finishing explanation batches
  with blanket approval of every executable rule.
## Remaining project quality work

- [ ] **Revisit borderline support-point judgment if play-throughs warrant it.**
  In particular, Pass versus game after a simple major raise may depend on whether
  responder's shortness is in opener's original suit; the current point thresholds
  remain unchanged, although the explanation now acknowledges the limitation.
- [ ] **Explanation integration review after the remaining explanation work.** Review
  rule buckets in manageable groups for existing explanation functions or evidence
  that do not reach normal feedback, unsupported-call feedback, recorded-call
  substitutions, or Report output.
- [ ] **User-facing explanation wording after the remaining explanation work.**
  Replace "the trainer" with "the app" and explain judgment limits in ordinary
  bridge language rather than internal scope terminology.
- [ ] **Ending and scope guidance.** Distinguish exercise completion from a finished
  auction; explain unresolved forcing or game-forcing obligations where relevant.
  Finish the focused audit of auction-stage recognition, agreement-sensitive meanings,
  and missing or misleading scope boundaries.
- [ ] **Final quality checks.** Review remaining substantive full-auction concerns
  using information available to the bidder at each turn, not the unseen hands.
  Check dealer orientation when it materially affects the lesson, guidance accuracy,
  and any interactions between revised deals and existing rule tests.
- [ ] **Final source and test cleanup.** Review naming, comments, organization,
  unnecessary wrappers, and readability without expanding test infrastructure.
