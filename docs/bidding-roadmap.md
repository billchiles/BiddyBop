# Bidding Coverage Roadmap

This is a short list of work remaining after introducing all-seat practice
and before final project cleanup. The executable bidding rules, practice corpus, and README
describe the selected bidding system and its deliberate scope limits; this file is
not a historical progress log or a commitment to model every possible auction.

## Agreed scope

- Generally teach openings, responses, opener rebids, and selected responder rebids;
  and overcalls/Doubles, advances, and selected overcaller/Doubler rebids. Later
  judgment-heavy bidding may end with auction-specific guidance.
- Do not create practice exercises merely to achieve numerical rule coverage.
  Preserve credible alternatives and prioritize useful instructional decisions.
- Use the same selected bidding agreements for all four seats, including the
  compact fixed DONT treatment against a strong 1NT opening.
- Practice deals have four fixed, complete hands and credible recorded calls for
  every seat. North is always dealer, but need not be opener. Exercise completion
  need not mean the actual bridge auction is over.
- Preserve the two practice auctions that end with three Passes: the final Pass
  is the actual tested balancing or overcaller-rebid decision, not filler.
- Keep quantitative rule/deal/explanation reach in `docs/by-the-numbers.md`,
  regenerated by `tests/stats.ts`. Human-reviewed coverage interpretation is
  deliberately brief and static in that generator; do not maintain a separate
  per-rule classification register or treat coverage counts as pass/fail goals.

## Remaining project quality work

- [ ] **Keep practice explanations within the bidder's available information.**
  In `responder-rebidding-convention-test-deals.ts`, 1NT–2D–2H–3NT
  (responder-rebid-after-nt-conventions, case 6), revise the South 3NT
  `practiceGuidance` note. Explain that a five-card heart transfer and balanced
  game strength offer opener the choice of 3NT or 4H with three-card support,
  without disclosing that opener actually holds only two hearts. Preserve
  decision-time knowledge and make this a wording-only cleanup, not a bidding
  eligibility or practice-auction change.
- [ ] **Coverage-guided stress testing beyond practice deals.**
  The November 2026 review found 111 executable rule IDs never qualifying a
  recorded call or alternative: 9 specialized slam treatments (low practice
  priority), 53 neighboring choices with qualifying siblings, and 49 other
  uncovered decisions. These are not scope boundaries, and the sibling count
  does not establish direct coverage. All 35 uncovered rules in the overcalling
  collection concern overcaller rebids (25 siblings, 2 specialized slam cases,
  8 others); do not prioritize blanket expansion of this family. Prefer targeted
  checks for substantive opener-rebid omissions, competitive/conventional
  continuations, and responder rebids after 2NT Stayman. Use seeded,
  reproducible hands and plausible auctions to inspect both qualifying and
  failed alternatives, agreement variants, and final student feedback.
  No automatic bidder, new rule-family inventory, coverage quota, or extra
  student practice deals merely to improve percentages. Add a practice deal
  only for a genuinely useful new teaching situation.
- [ ] **Revisit borderline support-point judgment if play-throughs warrant it.**
  In particular, Pass versus game after a simple major raise may depend on whether
  responder's shortness is in opener's original suit; the current point thresholds
  remain unchanged, although the explanation now acknowledges the limitation.
- [ ] **Final quality checks.** Review remaining substantive full-auction concerns
  using information available to the bidder at each turn, not the unseen hands.
  Check dealer orientation when it materially affects the lesson, guidance accuracy,
  and any interactions between revised deals and existing rule tests.
- [ ] **Final source and test cleanup.** Review naming, comments, organization,
  unnecessary wrappers, and readability without expanding test infrastructure.
