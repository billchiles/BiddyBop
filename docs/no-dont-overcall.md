## Bidding standard and decisions reached

**Primary standard:** Teach sound, commonly understood and reasonably intuitive bridge rather than a collection of expert or highly specialized treatments. Assess each unusual action's prevalence, clarity, incremental instructional value, forcing implications and implementation cost. Favor natural bids, ordinary fit/shape descriptions, useful Pass, and honest judgment boundaries. Do not confuse a 5–4 *holding* qualifying for a two-suit takeout Double with a convention promising exactly 5–4.

**Agreed direction:**

- Family A sandwich 1NT is **approved 16–18 actual HCP**, balanced, with basic stoppers in both naturally named opposing suits; it is **natural**, not artificial Sandwich NT. This numerical approval **does not automatically extend to B–E**. Do not invent another 1NT convention for a previously passed sandwich bidder.
- **Family A approved:** When opponents name *two* natural suits, sandwich takeout Double requires **12+ actual HCP and 4+ in each of the two unbid suits**; 5–4 qualifies; ordinary 4–3 does **not**. Both qualified suit/Double calls stay available with situation-specific instructional preference; full A4/A5 examples are recorded below. Family C/D instead consider **three-unbid-suit** takeout doubles with distinct hand tests, not this A predicate.
- **Do not implement a specialized opener Double** after sandwich suit interference in the initial scope: no implicit support Double, support Redouble, opponent's takeout meaning, or other artificial treatment.
- Todd's `3H` strong-spade-raise cuebid after `1C – Pass – 1H – 1S – 2H – ?` is **not approved**. Reconsider only with evidence it is common and worth teaching; otherwise prefer ordinary natural actions or a boundary. Do not prebuild its forcing continuations.
- Responder's **reopening/cooperative Double** after sandwich overcall and two Passes, e.g. `1D – Pass – 1H – 1S – Pass – Pass – Double`, is **explicitly deferred from the initial positive-rule inventory**: meaningful West +7/North +8 and penalty conversion are not modeled. Preserve a truthful judgment boundary and consistent coverage policy across A/C/D without conflating distinct meanings.
- All Family A main turns **+3 to +7 have accepted design direction**; remaining exact quantitative options (displaced competitive raises, a possible five-card natural minor repeat, reverses' forcing status, unusual game decisions) are **explicitly provisional** in the A integration checkpoint. User has **not** approved executable implementation; start a **new chat** before code generation.

**Pass and judgment:** A supported Pass, a plausible conservative alternative, and a hand beyond reliable judgment are different outcomes. Do not infer Pass when no positive rule qualifies. Do not assign a penalty Double without a declared treatment, or use a scope boundary as an endorsement of Pass. Competition can change the level and meaning of a call; do not mechanically elevate an unavailable uncontested rebid.
